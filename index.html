The Core Mission

What Problems Does This Project Solve?

Vehicle fleets (delivery vans, taxis, autonomous cars) rely on GPS data for dispatch and routing. But real-world GPS is notoriously noisy, drifting into buildings or jumping across parallel streets.

🗺️

1. Map Matching (History)

Taking raw, noisy $(\mathrm{lat}, \mathrm{lon}, t)$ GPS traces and deducing the exact sequence of road segments the vehicle physically traversed on the OpenStreetMap road graph.

// Observation Input:
[(41.157, -8.629, t₀), (41.158, -8.630, t₁), …]
// Matched Graph Trajectory:
[RoadSeg #14892 → RoadSeg #14893 → RoadSeg #15102]
🔮

2. Path Prediction (Future)

Forecasting where the vehicle will drive next into the future across horizons $h \in \{1, 5, 15\}$ steps without seeing future GPS points, by rolling out the latent world model's prior dynamics.

$$h_{t+1} = \operatorname{GRU}\big(h_t, [z_t^{\text{prior}}, a_t]\big)$$
Decision: $\hat{y}_{t+1} = \operatorname{argmax}_{k} \left( \mathbf{q}(h_{t+1}, z_{t+1})^\top \mathbf{k}(\text{candidate}_k) \right)$
⚡

The Breakthrough Discovery

RL alone loses to geometry (65% vs 84.5%). But RL + Geometry wins decisively (86.8% offline / 82.9% online)! World-model road belief scores fused with Newson-Krumm transition geometry form an unbeatable hybrid engine.

Theoretical Foundations

DreamerV3 & RSSM Papers: Bridge to AlphaEvolve

Understanding the mathematical mechanics of Hafner et al.'s Recurrent State-Space Models (RSSMs) and why porting DreamerV3 from pixel game worlds to road networks required a structural revolution.

The Architecture of a World Model

In traditional Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs/LSTMs), the hidden state is entirely deterministic. If the world has branching futures or ambiguous observations, deterministic states are forced to average across possibilities, causing blurriness and hallucination.

An RSSM (Recurrent State-Space Model) splits the representation into two cooperating parts:

1. Deterministic State $h_t$ (Memory)

A continuous vector ($h_t \in \mathbb{R}^{512}$) governed by a GRU Cell. It preserves long-term temporal context: velocity, current road class, route progression over the past several kilometers.

2. Stochastic Latent $z_t$ (Uncertainty)

A discrete categorical representation ($32 \times 32$ matrix). It captures immediate spatial ambiguity: "Did the vehicle turn left at the fork or continue straight?"

Core Mathematical Formulations

AlphaEvolve adopts the five stabilizing invariants introduced in the DreamerV3 paper (Hafner et al., 2023):

A. Discrete Straight-Through Categoricals

Instead of Gaussian latents (which suffer from variance collapse in complex spaces), DreamerV3 samples from $G = 32$ groups of $C = 32$ discrete categories. During forward pass, it takes a hard one-hot sample; during backward pass, it computes gradients with respect to the soft probabilities:

$$\mathbf{z} = \operatorname{one\_hot}\big(\operatorname{argmax}(\mathbf{p})\big) + \mathbf{p} - \operatorname{sg}(\mathbf{p})$$

Forward pass produces hard discrete state $\mathbf{z} \in \{0, 1\}^{32 \times 32}$; backward pass propagates gradients via the straight-through estimator $\nabla_{\mathbf{p}} \mathbf{z} \approx \mathbf{I}$.

B. 1% Unimix Floor (Anti-Collapse Stabilizer)

To prevent any single category from reaching probability $1.0$ (which would yield infinite $\log p$ and zero exploration), a 1% uniform distribution is blended into all logits:

$$p_{\text{mixed}} = (1 - \eta) \cdot \operatorname{softmax}(\boldsymbol{\ell}) + \eta \cdot \frac{1}{C}, \qquad \text{where } \eta = 0.01, \; C = 32$$

Guarantees a strictly positive entropy floor across all latent classes, preventing deterministic collapse $\log 0 \to -\infty$.

C. KL Balancing & Free Bits

The prior $p(z_t \mid h_t)$ trains toward the posterior $q(z_t \mid h_t, x_t)$ with weight $0.5$, while the posterior regularizes toward the prior with weight $0.1$. A free-bits floor of $1.0\,\text{nat}$ per group prevents the latent space from collapsing when the prior is inaccurate:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{KL}} = 0.5 \sum_{g=1}^G \max\left(1.0, \, \operatorname{KL}\big(\operatorname{sg}(q_g) \parallel p_g\big)\right) + 0.1 \sum_{g=1}^G \max\left(1.0, \, \operatorname{KL}\big(q_g \parallel \operatorname{sg}(p_g)\big)\right)$$

Asymmetric stop-gradient trains prior dynamics 5× faster than regularizing posterior latents, keeping representations informative.

D. Symlog & Symexp Transformations

Compresses wide dynamic ranges of physics rewards (e.g., one-way violation $-5.0$, heading alignment $+0.5$, topology $+2.0$) so gradients do not overwhelm representation learning:

$$\operatorname{symlog}(x) = \operatorname{sign}(x) \cdot \ln(1 + |x|), \qquad \operatorname{symexp}(y) = \operatorname{sign}(y) \cdot \big(\exp(|y|) - 1\big)$$

Preserves exact sign and near-zero gradient slopes while compressing extreme outliers without artificial sigmoid saturation.

The Posterior Collapse Mystery (Track A → Track C)

In the initial 3 runs (Track A), we built a faithful DreamerV3 port equipped with a Mixture Density Network (MDN) GPS reconstruction head. It suffered immediate, catastrophic posterior collapse.

Root Cause: Freeloading on the Deterministic Channel

As proven theoretically by Dai, Wang, & Wipf (ICML 2020), deep nonlinear decoders have bad local minima where the deterministic GRU state $h_t$ "freeloads" on teacher-forced road context. The network discovers it can minimize reconstruction loss via $h_t$ alone, setting the stochastic channel $z_t$ to uninformative noise ($\operatorname{KL} \to 0$).

The Solution: The Decoder-Light Architecture

Drawing from MuDreamer (Burchi & Timofte 2024) and TD-MPC2 (Hansen et al. 2024), we eliminated the heavy MDN continuous GPS reconstruction head entirely:

  • Z-Only Coarse GPS Decode: A linear probe $\mathcal{L}_{\text{gps}} = \|\operatorname{MLP}(z_t) - x_t^{\text{gps}}\|_2^2$ sees only $z_t$ (structurally blind to $h_t$). The deterministic state $h_t$ can no longer satisfy reconstruction.
  • Road Candidate Cross-Entropy as Primary Signal: Classifying candidate road segments replaces raw coordinate reconstruction, focusing representations entirely on discrete navigational choices.
  • Result: Posterior collapse vanished completely! KL divergence stabilized at a healthy $8\text{--}9+\,\mathrm{nats}$ in $16 \times 16$ and $22.8\text{--}23.6\,\mathrm{nats}$ in $32 \times 32$.

Curriculum

The Concepts Ladder: Basics to Advanced

Everything you need to master: from spatial coordinate frames and classical Hidden Markov Models to straight-through categorical estimators, Goodhart's law, and scaling laws.

Level 1: Foundational

GPS Noise, Multipath & Spatial Drift

Consumer and taxi GPS sensors have typical errors of 5–15 meters, ballooning to 50+ meters in urban canyons due to building reflections (multipath interference). Simple nearest-road snapping fails catastrophically at overpasses, dual carriageways, and intersections.

Level 1: Foundational

Dual Line Graph of Road Networks

Standard maps represent intersections as nodes and streets as edges. A Line Graph (Dual Graph) inverts this: each street segment becomes a node $u \in V$, and a directed edge connects $u \to v$ if and only if a vehicle can legally turn from segment $u$ into segment $v$. Immediate U-turns are pruned by construction.

Level 2: Intermediate Benchmark

Newson & Krumm (2009) Hidden Markov Model

The definitive classical baseline. Snapping each GPS fix to candidates within search radius $r = 50\,\mathrm{m}$ using two probability distributions:

1. Gaussian Emission Probability:
$$p(x_t \mid s_t = k) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma} \exp\left(-\frac{d_{\perp}(x_t, k)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right)$$
Where $d_{\perp}$ is perpendicular distance to road center, $\sigma = 10.0\,\mathrm{m}$.
2. Exponential Transition Penalty:
$$p(s_{t+1} = j \mid s_t = i) = \frac{1}{\beta} \exp\left(-\frac{|d_{\text{route}}(i, j) - d_{\text{gc}}(x_t, x_{t+1})|}{\beta}\right)$$
Where $d_{\text{route}}$ is shortest Dijkstra path, $d_{\text{gc}}$ is Haversine distance, $\beta = 20.0\,\mathrm{m}$.

Decoded globally via dynamic programming (Viterbi traceback). Achieves 84.47% offline accuracy, but drops to 59.18% online when greedy decoding without future lookahead.

Level 3: Advanced

Straight-Through Gradient Estimation

Sampling from discrete distributions has zero derivative almost everywhere. The straight-through estimator computes forward passes with the hard categorical sample $\mathbf{z}$, but routes the backward pass directly through the unimix-smoothed softmax probabilities $\mathbf{p}$, preserving clean backpropagation gradients: $\nabla_{\mathbf{p}} \mathbf{z} \approx \mathbf{I}$.

Level 3: Advanced

Goodhart's Law & RL Actor Collapse

When fine-tuning the RL actor policy (Stage 3) on imagined rollouts against a learned reward head, accuracy peaked at step 0 (77.4%) and degraded monotonically to ~65%. The policy learned to exploit blind spots in the frozen learned reward model rather than truly matching the road—a classic demonstration of Goodhart's Law / reward overoptimization (Gao et al., ICML 2023).

Level 3: Advanced

DAgger / Weak Supervision Relabeling

Nearest-point targets are topology-blind. By generating full-trajectory HMM Viterbi paths for 121.4M fixes and blending targets ($\alpha = 0.5$), the student model learns global continuity, cutting disconnected road jumps from 5.83% down to 4.71% without sacrificing accuracy.

Empirical Engineering

The Research Drama: What Didn't Work and Why

Real research logs document failures with as much rigor as successes. These negative results closed unproductive directions and forged the final production system.

Track A: Faithful DreamerV3 RSSM Collapses Runs 1–3

Failure: Ported DreamerV3 with an MDN (Mixture Density Network) continuous GPS reconstruction head. Posterior collapsed across 4 consecutive experiment rounds. KL dropped to ~0 nats.
Diagnosis: Deterministic GRU state $h_t$ freeloaded on teacher-forced road context via the deep decoder (Dai et al. ICML 2020).
Resolution: Abandoned heavy reconstruction; created Track C (Decoder-Light).

RL Actor Fails to Beat Supervised Road Head Stage 3 Probe

Failure: Discrete actor trained via imagined rollouts achieved only 65% matching accuracy, far below the simple supervised road head (77%+).
Diagnosis: Early-stopping probe proved Step 0 was the peak; every gradient step degraded accuracy monotonically. Classic Goodhart's law against an imperfect learned reward head.
Resolution: RL actor track closed with formal proof; production engine standardized on road-head decode.

Learned Road Semantics Fail Out-of-Distribution Porto → Beijing T-Drive

Failure: Zero-shot testing of a Porto-trained model on Beijing dropped 17 percentage points in accuracy. The hybrid model scored 0.7871 vs pure geometry 0.7955.
Diagnosis: Classical geometry (great-circle distances, road topology) transfers universally; learned semantic embeddings overfit to the layout and conventions of the training city.
Resolution: Engineered automatic city-mode fallback: trained cities use Hybrid Mode, unseen cities automatically use pure geometric HMM.

Decoder-Light Architecture & Hybrid Viterbi Win Run-2-XL & Research2

Breakthrough: Stripping MDN and gating GPS decode to $z$-only cured collapse permanently. Fusing world-model road probabilities into the Newson-Krumm Viterbi matrix broke all previous records: 86.8% offline accuracy and 82.9% streaming online accuracy!

Neural Scaling Laws Confirmed P1 Data & P2 Capacity

Breakthrough:
• Data Scaling: 200k (75.6%) → 400k (77.7%) → 800k (79.7%) → 1.66M (81.0%): steady +2.0pp per doubling of training trajectories.
• Capacity Scaling: Widening discrete latents from $16 \times 16$ to $32 \times 32$ (matching DreamerV3 standard width) delivered an immediate $+2.5\text{ to }+3.3\,\mathrm{pp}$ accuracy leap at only 58% of training schedule!

Running on Kaggle: Fast Reference (self_guide.md)

The project trained across chained 12-hour Kaggle T4x2 GPU sessions using 9 private datasets. Essential operational rules:

Windows Charmap Crash Guard:

Always export PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8 and PYTHONUTF8=1, otherwise Kaggle CLI silent-truncates logs to 0 bytes.

Resume Chaining:

Runs save checkpoints every 1,000 steps and reseed alphaevolve-ckpt, picking up seamlessly on weekly GPU quota resets.

Candidate Cache MMap:

82.4M-row candidate arrays are memmapped from disk to keep resident RAM under the strict 30GB Kaggle ceiling.
